## The tournament

The PGN holds the first four boards of each round — 20 games — so the standings below are those of the games in the file, not the official ones. A player who played on a lower board in a round has a dash there: no game in the file, not a bye.

Krivoborodov and Moritz Stoettner both finished on 4 points from 5; Krivoborodov is ranked first on Buchholz. Korneev and Milov followed on 3.5.
