## The tournament

Nodirbek Yakubboev won with 6 points from 7, a point and a half ahead of the field. Vladislav Artemiev and Nodirbek Abdusattorov shared second place on 4.5, Artemiev ahead on Sonneborn-Berger, and Yagiz Kaan Erdogmus was fourth on 4.
