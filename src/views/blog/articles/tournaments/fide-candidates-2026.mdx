## The tournament

Javokhir Sindarov took first place with 10 points from 14, a point and a half clear of Anish Giri on 8.5. Fabiano Caruana was third with 7.5, ahead of Wei Yi on 7 and Hikaru Nakamura on 6.5. Matthias Bluebaum and R Praggnanandhaa finished level on 6, Bluebaum ahead on Sonneborn-Berger, and Andrey Esipenko was last with 4.5.
