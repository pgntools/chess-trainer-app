The 46th Chess Olympiad, played in Samarkand, Uzbekistan, from 16 September 2026: two team events side by side, the Open and the Women's, each a Swiss of eleven rounds. Every country sends a team of four boards, and the hosts fielded more than one. The Open had 206 teams and the Women's 189: 8,645 games in all.

## The format

Two teams meet on four boards at once, and every game two teams play in a round is one match. A match is won on **board points**, the games' points summed, and is worth **match points**: 2 for a win, 1 for a draw. The tables rank by match points, then board points. Where two teams are level on both, they are listed by name; the Olympiad breaks such ties its own way, so its official order may differ.

Every game in these files names the federation of each player. Where all of a team's players share one, as a national team's do, its flag stands after its name. Belarus has none: its players are listed under FIDE's flag (`FID`).

## The Open

Uzbekistan, the hosts, won with 20 match points: ten wins, and one loss, 1½–2½ to Germany in round 8. Germany and India followed on 18, level on board points too.
