A repertoire answers every move the way the trainer would: the wider the arrow, the likelier the reply. Each board faces the side its repertoire is played from.

## The markup

```mdx
<BoardRow>
  <RepertoireBoard _id="/repertoires/<id>" fallback="e4-white" startMove="1" />
  <RepertoireBoard _id="/repertoires/<id>" fallback="caro-kann-black" startMove="1..." />
</BoardRow>
```

- `_id` is the repertoire's address, `/repertoires/<id>`. The two above are placeholders, so both boards show their fallback.
- `fallback` is a sample that ships with the app — `e4-white` or `caro-kann-black` — shown, marked as a sample, wherever the repertoire is not. Repertoires live on the reader's own device, so on anyone else's the address names nothing.
- Without a `fallback`, a missing repertoire says so in place of its board.
