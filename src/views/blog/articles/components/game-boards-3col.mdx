## The markup

```mdx
<BoardRow>
  <CollectionGameBoard game="/library/fischer/50" startMove="17" />
  <CollectionGameBoard game="/library/fischer/908" startMove="7" />
  <CollectionGameBoard game="/library/capablanca/442" startMove="8" />
</BoardRow>
```

- `game` is the game's address — the one its own board has in the address bar: `/library/<collection>/<number>`.
- `startMove` is where the board opens — see [Where a board opens](/blog/components/start-move).
- `<BoardRow>` puts its children side by side, as many columns as it has children, and stacks them on a narrow screen.

A shipped collection's game is there for every reader. A game of an uploaded collection is only on the device it was uploaded on; anywhere else the board says the game is not here.
