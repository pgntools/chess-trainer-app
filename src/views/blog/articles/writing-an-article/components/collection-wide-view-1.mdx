## The markup

```mdx
<BoardRow>
  <CollectionCard _id="/library/fischer" showGame="52" />
</BoardRow>
```

- `_id` is the collection's address, `/library/<collection>`.
- `showGame` is the game on the board, by its number in the collection — the first when absent.
- `rows` is how many games the table shows at a time (8 by default) and `startMove` where the first board opens — see [the second view](/blog/writing-an-article/components/collection-wide-view-2).

The card reads the collection's index for its table and its games for the board, the Library's own files, fetched once.
