`<NavCards>` is every screen of the app as a card, section by section — what the landing page was before it was written in MDX. It is built from the sidebar's own list, so a screen added there appears here with no edit.

<NavCards headingLevel={3} />

## The markup

```mdx
<NavCards headingLevel={3} />
```

`headingLevel` is the sections' heading level: `2` by default, `3` under a heading of the page's own.
