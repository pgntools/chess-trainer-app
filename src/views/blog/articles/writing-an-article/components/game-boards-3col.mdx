## The markup

```mdx
<BoardRow>
  <InlinePgnGame2colV src="/library/fischer/50" start="17" />
  <InlinePgnGame2colV src="/library/fischer/908" start="7" />
  <InlinePgnGame2colV src="/library/capablanca/442" start="8" />
</BoardRow>
```

- `src` is the game's address — the one its own board has in the address bar: `/library/<collection>/<number>`.
- `start` is where the board opens — see [Where a board opens](/blog/writing-an-article/components/start-move).
- `<InlinePgnGame2colV>` puts the moves under the board, in numbered pairs, in a box half its height — the one for boards side by side.
- `<BoardRow>` puts its children side by side, as many columns as it has children, and stacks them on a narrow screen.

A shipped collection's game is there for every reader. A game of an uploaded collection is only on the device it was uploaded on; anywhere else the board says the game is not here.
