`startMove` is where a board opens. `<RepertoireBoard>` and `<CollectionCard>` take it; a game's board — `<InlinePgnGame>`, `<InlinePgnGame2colH>`, `<InlinePgnGame2colV>` — calls it `start`, and reads it the same way.

## The forms it takes

- A move number walks the game's mainline. `"4"` (or `"4."`) is the position after White's 4th move; `"4..."` (or `"...4"`) after Black's 4th; `"0"` the start. Past the end of the game, the board opens at its end.
- A line of moves follows them — numbered (`"1. c4 c5 2. Nc3"`) or bare (`"c4 c5 Nc3"`) — and may go into a side line of a repertoire. Where the tree stops following the line, the board opens at the last move it does hold.
- Absent, or words it cannot read: the start.

The board's "Back to the start" goes to the game's own start, wherever it opened.

## Without the arrows

An arrow on the board gives the next move away. For a position the reader should think about first, `showNextMoveArrow={false}` leaves the arrows off; the move is still in the list under the board.
