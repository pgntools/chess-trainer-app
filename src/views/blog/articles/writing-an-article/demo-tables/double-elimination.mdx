A **double-elimination knockout**: a player is out only after losing two matches. Every match lost in the **winners' bracket** drops its loser into the **losers' bracket**, where a second loss ends their event. The example is the play-in of the Esports World Cup 2026, eight players in Paris on 11 August.

## The format

The Week in Chess numbers the winners' bracket's rounds from 1 and the losers' bracket's from 51. The embed is told where the second bracket starts: `losersFromRound="51"`. Each bracket's rounds are then numbered from 1.

Wei Yi won the winners' bracket, beating Vladislav Artemiev in its final. Andrey Esipenko lost his first match to Wei, then won four in a row in the losers' bracket, the last against Artemiev, who had dropped down from the winners' final.

## The brackets

The winners' bracket over the losers', each a column per round. A screen reader hears each bracket's name after the event's.

## The final stage: a knockout with a match for third place

The event's final stage, eight players from 14 August, was a plain knockout, so its PGN takes no `losersFromRound`. Its last round holds two matches. The final: Magnus Carlsen beat Denis Lazavik 6–2 over two four-game parts, which the bracket counts as one match. And the match for third place, between the two beaten semi-finalists: Hikaru Nakamura beat Alireza Firouzja. The bracket finds that match itself, since both its players lost the round before, and sets it under the final with a caption.
