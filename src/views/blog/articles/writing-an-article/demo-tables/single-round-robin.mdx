A **single round robin**: every player meets every other once. Eight players make seven rounds and 28 games. The example is the Green Hills Resort Masters 2026, a rapid event.

## The format

As in a double round robin, ties are broken by Sonneborn-Berger: the points of the opponents a player beat, plus half the points of those they drew with. Buchholz is left out, because everyone's opponents are nearly the same.

## The crosstable

The same table as a double round robin's: a row and a column per player, in rank order. Each cell holds one result, since each pair met once.

The component does not need to be told whether the event was single or double: it puts every game between two players in their cell.
