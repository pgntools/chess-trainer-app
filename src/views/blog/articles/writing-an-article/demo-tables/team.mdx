A **team event**: teams, not players, meet. Every board of one team plays the same board of the other at once. The examples are the FIDE World Rapid and Blitz Team Championships 2026 in Hong Kong: a Swiss of teams for the rapid, and a knockout of teams for the blitz final stage.

## The format

Each game names its teams in two tags, `WhiteTeam` and `BlackTeam`, and every game two teams play in a round is one match. A match is won on **board points**, the games' points summed. In a Swiss of teams, a won match is worth 2 **match points** and a drawn one 1, as at the Olympiad. Teams are ranked by match points, then board points.

These files tag no player's federation, so no team here has a flag. Where every player of a team shares one, as at an Olympiad, the team's flag stands after its name: see the [46th Chess Olympiad](/blog/tournaments/olympiad-2026).

## The rapid: a Swiss of teams

48 teams, twelve rounds, six boards. A round's cell is the team's board points in that match, toned as the match went. A screen reader hears the opponent and the score both ways, for example "Round 1 against …: 4½–1½, won". Dragon Chilling won on 18 match points, ahead of Hexamind Chess Team and Team MGD1, also on 18, on board points.

## The blitz final stage: a knockout of teams

Sixteen teams. Each match is played in legs, all six boards at once, and a leg is won on board points. The bracket gives each team the legs it won (½ for a drawn leg), with its board points after them in brackets. The last column is the final, in which Dragon Chilling beat Endgame.AI, with the match for third place under it, captioned: Uzbekistan won it over Hexamind Chess Team.
