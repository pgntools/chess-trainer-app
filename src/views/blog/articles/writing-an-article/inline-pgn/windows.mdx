A window is a stretch of the game's mainline: `from` its first position, `to` its last, `start` where the board opens. The reader can go back no further than `from` and on no further than `to`. These boards show Rubinstein – Capablanca, San Sebastian 1911 — the game of the [review](/blog/writing-an-article/inline-pgn/rubinstein-capablanca-1911).

## The whole game

With no window, the board opens at the start and goes to the end.

`"5"` is the position after White's 5th move, `"15"` after White's 15th, `"11"` after White's 11th — so the board opens with Black to answer 11. Nxe6.
