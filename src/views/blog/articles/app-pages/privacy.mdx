Last updated: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what chessapp.dev (the "App") does with information about you. The short version: **the App has no accounts and no server-side database. Your games, analyses, repertoires and settings stay in your browser, on your device, and are never sent to us.** The only personal data we may receive is what you send us yourself, by email.

## What the App stores on your device

Everything below lives in your own browser and is read only by the App running in that browser. We receive none of it.

**Browser storage (`localStorage`) — your preferences:**

- `chessapp.theme` — the look you chose in Settings → Appearance.
- `chessapp.engine` — the chess engine you chose in Settings → Engine.
- `i18nextLng` — the language you chose (English or Hebrew).
- `mui-mode`, with `mui-color-scheme-light` and `mui-color-scheme-dark` — light, dark or system colour mode. The MUI interface library keeps these.

**Session storage (`sessionStorage`) — until you close the tab:**

- `chessapp.developmentNoticeDismissed` — that you closed the in-development notice, so it is not shown again in the same session.

**Local databases (IndexedDB) — your chess data:**

- `chessapp.engine` — the games you played against the engine.
- `chessapp.analyses` — your saved analysis boards and their folders.
- `chessapp.repertoires` — your opening repertoires.
- `chessapp.library` — the game collections you added to the Library.

These records hold whatever you put into them: PGN files, comments, the names of players and events. If they contain personal data, about you or about others, it stays on your device and is under your control.

The App sets **no cookies**. See the [Cookies Notice](/cookies) for the details.

## What we do not do

- No accounts, no sign-in, no profile.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party scripts, fonts or images: everything the App loads comes from the site it is served from.
- The Stockfish chess engine runs inside your browser, in a local Web Worker. Your positions are not sent anywhere to be analysed.
- We do not sell or share your data, because we do not receive it.

## Why the App stores these items, and on what basis

Each item is **strictly necessary** for something you ask the App to do: remember your settings, show it in your language and look, and keep the games and analyses you save between visits. The EU ePrivacy Directive (Article 5(3)) and the UK's PECR let a site store such items on your device without asking for consent, and Israeli law sets no consent rule for them. So the App shows no consent banner. Nothing is stored about you beyond what you do in the App, and none of it reaches us, so the GDPR's legal bases for our own processing do not come into play for this storage.

## Hosting and server logs

The App is a static website served by **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc.) and **Azure Static Web Apps** (Microsoft). Like any web host, they may keep ordinary server logs of each request — typically your IP address, the time, the address requested and your browser's user-agent — to operate and secure their service. We do not receive or analyse these logs. The hosts may process them outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their own policies: [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). Where we are responsible for this processing, its legal basis is our legitimate interest in delivering and securing the site (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

## When you write to us

If you email us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and your message. We use them only to answer you and handle your request — the legal basis is our legitimate interest in replying (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), or the steps you ask us to take — and we keep them only as long as that takes, unless the law requires otherwise. We do not add you to any mailing list.

## Links to other sites

The footer's *Source* link and the Support tab's issue link lead to GitHub; some articles link to other chess sites. Once you follow a link, that site's own policy applies. **Anything you write in a GitHub issue is public.**

## Your control over your data

- **Download it:** Settings → Export gives you all your data as one zip file, and Settings → Import puts such a zip back.
- **See how much there is:** Settings → Storage.
- **Delete it:** remove records inside the App, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings (usually under Privacy → Site data, or "Clear cookies and site data", which removes local storage and the databases too). Clearing it cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.
- Your browser may also clear this data on its own — in a private window when you close it, or when your device runs short of space. Export what you want to keep.

## Your rights

Because none of your App data reaches us, there is usually nothing for us to give, correct or erase — you hold it, in your browser. Where we do hold personal data about you (for example, an email you sent us), you have, under the **EU and UK GDPR**, the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. Under Israel's **Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, as amended by Amendment 13**, you have the right to review information held about you, to ask for its correction or deletion, and to complain to the Privacy Protection Authority. To use any of these rights, write to us at the address below. We answer within one month.

## Children

The App collects no personal information from anyone, children included. Children may use it like anyone else; a child who wants to write to us should ask a parent first.
