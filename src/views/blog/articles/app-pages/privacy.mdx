## What the App stores on your device

Everything below lives in your own browser and is read only by the App running in that browser. We receive none of it.

**Browser storage (`localStorage`) — your preferences:**

- `chessapp.theme` — the look you chose in Settings → Appearance.
- `chessapp.engine` — the chess engine you chose in Settings → Engine.
- `i18nextLng` — the language you chose (English or Hebrew).
- `mui-mode` (and the `mui-color-scheme-light` / `mui-color-scheme-dark` keys MUI keeps with it) — light, dark or system colour mode.

**Session storage (`sessionStorage`) — until you close the tab:**

- `chessapp.developmentNoticeDismissed` — remembers that you closed the in-development notice, so it is not shown again in the same session.

**Local databases (IndexedDB) — your chess data:**

- `chessapp.engine` — the games you played against the engine.
- `chessapp.analyses` — your saved analysis boards and their folders.
- `chessapp.repertoires` — your opening repertoires.
- `chessapp.library` — the game collections you added to the Library.

These records can contain whatever you put into them: PGN files, comments, names of players and events. If you enter personal data in them, it stays on your device.

The App sets **no cookies**. See the [Cookies Notice](/cookies) for the details.

## What we do not do

- No accounts, no sign-in, no profile.
- No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party scripts, fonts or images: everything the App loads comes from its own host.
- The Stockfish chess engine runs inside your browser, in a local Web Worker. Your positions are not sent anywhere to be analysed.
- We do not sell or share your data, because we do not have it.

## Why we use the storage, and on what basis

Storing these items is **strictly necessary** to provide the features you ask for: keeping your games and settings between visits, and showing the App in the language and look you chose. Under the GDPR and the ePrivacy rules, storage that is strictly necessary for a service you requested needs no consent. We therefore show no consent banner. You stay in control: nothing is stored about you beyond what you do in the App.

## Hosting and server logs

The App is a static website served by **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc.) and **Azure Static Web Apps** (Microsoft). Like any web host, they may keep ordinary server logs of each request — typically your IP address, the time, the address requested and your browser's user-agent — for security and operation. We do not receive or analyse these logs for any purpose of our own. Their handling is governed by the hosts' own policies: [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). The legal basis for this processing by the hosts, and by us where it applies, is legitimate interest in delivering and securing the site (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

## Your control over your data

- **Download it:** Settings → Export gives you all your data as one zip file, and Settings → Import puts such a zip back.
- **See how much there is:** Settings → Storage.
- **Delete it:** remove records inside the App, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings (usually under Privacy → Site data, or "Clear cookies and site data" — which also removes local storage and databases). Clearing it cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on our side, there is usually nothing for us to give, correct or erase — you hold it, in your browser. Where we do hold personal data (for example, if you write to us), you have, under the **EU/UK GDPR**: the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and to object, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. Under Israel's **Protection of Privacy Law, 1981, as amended by Amendment 13**, you have the right to review information held about you, to ask for its correction or deletion, and to complain to the Privacy Protection Authority. To use any of these rights, write to the address above.
