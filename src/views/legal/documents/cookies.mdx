**chessapp.dev sets no cookies.** It does store some data in your browser using other technologies — local storage, session storage and local databases. This notice lists all of them, so you can see exactly what the App keeps on your device and why.

## What a cookie is, and what the App uses instead

A cookie is a small file a website asks your browser to keep and to send back with every request to that site. The App uses none. Instead it uses your browser's own storage, which stays on your device and is **not sent to any server**: it is read only by the App, in your browser.

## Everything the App stores

**Local storage** (preferences):

- `chessapp.theme` — the look you picked, to show the App the way you chose.
- `chessapp.engine` — the chess engine you picked, to run the engine you chose.
- `i18nextLng` — your language, to show the App in it.
- `mui-mode` (and `mui-color-scheme-light` / `mui-color-scheme-dark`) — light, dark or system colour mode, to show the App in it.

**Session storage** (until you close the tab):

- `chessapp.developmentNoticeDismissed` — that you closed the in-development notice, so it is not shown again until you close the tab.

**IndexedDB** (your chess data):

- `chessapp.engine` — the games you played against the engine.
- `chessapp.analyses` — your saved analysis boards.
- `chessapp.repertoires` — your opening repertoires.
- `chessapp.library` — the collections you added to the Library.

## Why no consent banner

All of this is **strictly necessary** to give you the features you ask for: remembering your settings and keeping your own games and analyses between visits. Storage that is strictly necessary for a service you requested does not need your consent under the GDPR and the ePrivacy rules, or under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law. The App uses no analytics, advertising or tracking storage of any kind, so there is nothing to opt in or out of.

## How long it stays

Local storage and the databases stay until you delete them. Session storage disappears when you close the tab.

## How to see, export and delete it

- **Export:** Settings → Export downloads your data as one zip; Settings → Import brings it back.
- **See the size:** Settings → Storage.
- **Delete:** remove records in the App, or clear this site's data in your browser's settings. Look for "Site data", "Cookies and site data" or "Storage" in your browser's privacy settings — clearing it removes the local storage and the databases too, and cannot be undone.
